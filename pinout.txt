ESP32-S3 Pin Connections for 4.2" e-Paper Display
===================================================

Signal       | ESP32-S3 Pin
-------------|-------------
GND          | GND
SCL (SCK)    | 12
DC           | 9
BUSY         | 3
VCC          | 3.3V
SDA (MOSI)   | 11
CS (SS)      | 10
RES (RST)    | 4
